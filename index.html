1. Overview

Hex Hamlet (the "App", package kr.hicctv.hexhamlet) is a puzzle game played without an account. We do not run a server, there is no sign-in, and the App does not send game data to us. The App requests no dangerous permissions (no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or storage access).

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Game progress and settings: islands played, scores, unlocks, language, sound and graphics settings.
  • Play log: a record of your last 60 islands (score, time, moves). It leaves the device only if you tap "Export play log" in Settings and share or paste it yourself.

This data is never sent to our servers (we have none). If your device's Google Backup is on, it is included in your own Google account backup and device transfer; we cannot see it.

3. Data collected by third parties

The App shows ads with the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google. The App itself does not store or receive these values.

4. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) (ad consent): users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent form and can change their choice anytime under "Privacy options" in Settings. About UMP
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchase): Google Payments Privacy Notice

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads). We do not sell your information or share it with anyone other than the services above.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

6. Deleting your data

All game data is on your device. Clear the App's data (Settings > Apps > Hex Hamlet > Storage > Clear data) or uninstall the App to delete it. Copies in Google Backup can be managed in your device's backup settings. We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data held by Google is governed by Google's policies above.
